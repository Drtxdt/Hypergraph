# beta.9: reading and discovery

The image-free homepage merges matching site and home titles into one printable identity and gives featured writing the primary visual surface. Continuous article text sits on feathered paper, with elongated ridges and valleys at the viewport edges.

Printing retains its 1600ms timeline after the intro. Wordmarks use the configured display font and theme colors; photos retain a stable image beneath a material scan. Offscreen targets settle immediately.

Search adds optional dates, categories and safe keyword emphasis. Use the arrow keys to select a result and Enter to open it. Older search indexes remain supported. Mobile music starts collapsed and retains a mini player after intentional playback.

Article opening covers and music remain disabled by default. Existing contour, artwork, intro, motion and laser_print configuration continues to apply.
