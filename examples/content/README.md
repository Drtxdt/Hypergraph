# Representative content

Copy `source/` into an isolated Hexo site to test real photographic media alongside the regular demonstration. Do not overwrite a personal blog.

- `portrait.jpg`: NASA, Mae C. Jemison, S92-40463. Public domain U.S. government work. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mae_Carol_Jemison.jpg). Wikimedia's 500 px derivative, otherwise unchanged. Used as a portrait composition sample, not a site author or endorsement.
- `landscape.jpg`: National Park Service Digital Image Archives, GRCA9862. Public domain U.S. government work. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grand_Canyon_National_Park_GRCA9862.jpg). Wikimedia's 960 px derivative, otherwise unchanged. No endorsement implied.
- `terminal.svg`: original Hypergraph code illustration, MIT, same license as this repository.

Keep subjects within the right two-thirds of a desktop hero when choosing a focal point. Mobile stacks the image above text. For screenshot text that must remain entirely readable, put the full image in the article body rather than relying on the cropped cover. `cover_position` controls the focal point; `post.cover_mode` controls the article cover, not the homepage composition.
